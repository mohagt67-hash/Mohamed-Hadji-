Summary

Background

The war began on 7 October 2023, after a Hamas-led attack on southern Israel that Israeli officials say killed about 1,200 people, mostly civilians, and led to 252 hostages being taken. Israel responded with an air campaign and a large ground operation in Gaza.

Toll

Gaza’s Health Ministry and Israeli authorities report about 73,919 Palestinians and 2,046 Israelis killed as of 27 September 2026. The ministry does not separate civilians from combatants. The UN has described its figures as reliable.

Destruction

UNOSAT’s September 2024 assessment found that two-thirds of Gaza’s structures, 163,778 in total, were damaged. This is the latest assessment found, and the current figure is likely higher.

Ceasefire

A US-brokered ceasefire began in October 2025. It called for Hamas to hand power to a Palestinian committee overseen by the Board of Peace, and for phased disarmament linked to a phased Israeli withdrawal. Implementation has stalled. Gaza’s Health Ministry says 1,613 people have been killed since it began, as of 9 November. Israel says it targets people accused of violating the deal, and five Israeli soldiers have been killed in the same period.

Humanitarian situation

A storm flooded about 70,000 tents this weekend, and a landslide at a camp in Deir el-Balah killed five displaced people. Israel says more than 91,000 tents entered before winter, but it will not allow heavy equipment, and says reconstruction can begin only after Hamas disarms. The UN says no hospital in Gaza is fully functioning. The Board of Peace called Israel’s response to the flooding insufficient.

Main points of dispute

Who is responsible for ceasefire violations, whether disarmament must come before reconstruction, and how the war should be described legally.

Sources: Al Jazeera, CNN, AP, AFP, UNOSAT, Gaza Health Ministry and Israeli authorities. Entries are still being checked against primary sources.

    How we verify

    Two sources, minimum

    A claim is marked Verified only when two independent sources support it. Otherwise it stays Draft or Disputed.

    Media is checked first

    Photos and video are geolocated and dated before they appear. Material we cannot place is not published.

    Sources are named

    Every statistic names who reported it and when. Corrections are listed publicly with the date of change.
